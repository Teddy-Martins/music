Lesson four

Your first chords

A triad is three notes stacked in thirds: root, third, and fifth. Learn the shape once and you can move it to any white key to get a new major or minor chord.

C major and C minor

These two triads share a root and a fifth — only the third note changes, which is what turns "bright" into "dark."

Practice routine

Play C, then F, then G, then back to C, holding each for four counts. Once that's smooth, try it with your left hand while your right hand plays the melody of a simple song you already know.
